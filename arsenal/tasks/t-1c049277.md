When `lastRenderedPageBreak` marks cover the document, `page.estimate` uses them: `page_count == marks + 1` and every paragraph before the last mark gets its rendered page; the word-count model only fills paragraphs after the last mark or documents without marks. On a real 74k-word manuscript with 259 marks the estimate today says 397 pages where Word rendered 260.
