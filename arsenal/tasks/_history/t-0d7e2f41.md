Requested by literary-critic (so it can drop its .odt converter). A `.odt` path is read as an in-memory document (`doc_control/ooxml/odt.py`): every `text:p`/`text:h` of the body in order (lists, tables, sections, table of contents included); spans, links and fields read through; `text:s` as spaces, `text:tab` as a tab, `text:line-break` as a line break; headings keep their outline level as Heading 1-9; other paragraphs keep their paragraph style's display name (automatic styles resolved to the parent). Footnotes, comments, frames/text boxes and tracked deletions are left out, as in Word's main story. Writes refuse it (`UNSUPPORTED_CONTENT`); encrypted or non-text OpenDocument files are `NOT_A_DOCX`.
