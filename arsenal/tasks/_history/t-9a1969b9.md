Imported from issue #226

Found in the review of #225 (STORIES T10).

`revision.accept` and `revision.reject` skip row-level revisions (`w:trPr/w:ins` and `w:trPr/w:del`). Accept-all over a tracked deleted row, or reject-all over a tracked inserted row, leaves an empty row that is still marked.

**Fix:** treat row revisions as revisions in accept and reject.
- Accepting a deleted row, or rejecting an inserted one, removes the `w:tr`.
- Accepting an inserted row, or rejecting a deleted one, drops the marker.

**Gate:** a test in `tests/ops/` showing that accept-all and reject-all over a row insert and a row delete leave no row marker and no empty rows.

Size: S.

uv run pytest -q tests/ops/test_row_revisions.py -k test_accept_reject_all_resolve_row_revisions
