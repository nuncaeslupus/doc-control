Requested by literary-critic (its T6 structure; items 1-2 of its list, both blocking).
1. A paragraph whose style name holds a scene word as a whole word (profile `scene_style_words`, default after, tras, después; `scene_styles` switches it off) opens a scene (signal `scene_style`), unless it follows a heading or a scene-break paragraph, or the style is a body style.
2. An untitled heading (`-I-`, `Capítulo 3`) takes the short line right under it (at most 8 words, no final punctuation, no dialogue) as its title: chapters and parts report `heading_last`, and the line is not text. Decorated numerals (`-I-`, `— 3 —`, `[IV]`) are numerals. `heading_subtitles` switches it off.
