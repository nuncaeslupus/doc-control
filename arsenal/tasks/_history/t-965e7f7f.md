Requested by literary-critic: a cold `api.open` + `para.get` + `locate(at)` + `page.estimate(at)` on a 100k-word docx took ≈ 0.9 s on their machine. Operations are now imported one module at a time (`registry.get_operation`, `OPERATION_MODULES`); the Unicode mark tables of `model.search` are built on first search (~40 ms off every import); `xml.sax.saxutils`, which pulls in `urllib`/`http.client` (~30 ms), is replaced by `ooxml/xmltext.py` with identical output; `locate` takes `structure: false` to skip chapter/part/scene detection (those fields come back null). Page estimation stays whole-document (an incremental estimate would disagree with the full one).
