Imported from issue #232

Left open by the reviews of #228 (LAYOUT T7, `toc.insert` / `fields.update`):

- **RISK, `ooxml/soffice.py`:** on Windows a timeout runs `proc.kill()`, which stops only the `soffice.exe` launcher and can leave `soffice.bin` running. Kill the whole tree with `taskkill /T /F` or a job object.
- **NOTE:** the process is never reaped when the bounded 10s wait after the kill also times out. Nothing kills the process group when `communicate` is interrupted either.
- **NOTE:** REF fields take LibreOffice's text, not Word's. A REF to a missing bookmark becomes "Error: Reference source not found", and a `REF \n` to an unnumbered paragraph becomes empty. Both are written as tracked changes. Document this among the limits, or skip these results.
- **NOTE:** clearing `w:dirty` is not tracked, so reject-all does not restore the flag (undo does).
- **Earlier leftovers:**
  - no sandboxing of LibreOffice against hostile documents or link updates;
  - `toc.insert` uses whole-file undo;
  - TOC entry styling;
  - one weak test.
